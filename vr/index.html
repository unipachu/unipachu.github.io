VR Projects

A collection of VR projects focused on interaction systems and physics.

Unity VR Prototype

Role: Solo developer

Developed a VR research prototype comparing physics-based grabbing systems implemented with both Unity GameObjects (PhysX) and DOTS ECS (Unity Physics), enabling direct comparison of architecture, performance, and interaction behavior.

Technologies: Unity 6.3, C#, XR Interaction Toolkit, PhysX (GameObjects), DOTS ECS with Unity Physics.

Chaos Vehicle VR Template

Role: Solo developer

Developed a VR-enabled vehicle prototype using Unreal Engine's Chaos Vehicles system, featuring multiple input methods, 6DOF motion platform integration, and an in-game miniature (shown in the GIF) that visualizes real-world motion platform movement for testing and debugging.

Technologies: Unreal Engine 5.7, C++, Blueprints, Chaos Vehicles, VR.
